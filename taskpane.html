Your email signature

Your first email in a thread gets the full signature; later replies get the one-line version.

Smart formatting

Type naturally. These patterns turn into designed elements when you send.

A short line in bold on its own.

Start a line with Action: Decision: Deadline: Important: Ask: Note: FYI: or Next steps:

Percentages and amounts such as 6% -4% $1.2M get a marker.

Paste a link on its own line, such as a Power BI or SharePoint link.

[On track] [At risk] [Off track] [Done] [Blocked] [In progress]

One line, metrics split by |: KPI: Spend $4.1M -8% [On track] | Margin 18%. Arrows show the direction; add a status tag when you want green, amber or red.

Two or more lines in a row that start with a time: 10:00 Welcome 10:15-10:45 Q3 review

Before sending

If your email says "attached" but nothing is, Send asks first. A shared SharePoint or OneDrive link counts as attached.

Subject tags

  • [review] pauses once so you can check the styled email, then sends with a clean subject.
  • [plain] sends the email exactly as typed.
