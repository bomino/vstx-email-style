Versatex Email Style

An Outlook add-in that gives every email and meeting invite you send the Versatex look, automatically, when you select Send.

What you get

  • Brand frameThe Versatex bar on top and your signature at the bottom; a one-line signature on later replies in a thread.
  • Tidy lists and tablesHexagon bullets, yellow number badges, branded tables.
  • Smart formattingHeadings, callouts, KPI tiles, timed agendas, link buttons and status tags from simple typing.
  • Attachment reminderIf you write "attached" but forgot the file, Send asks first.

Install it (about 3 minutes)

  1. Download the add-in file.
    Download versatex-email-style.xml
  2. Open the add-ins page: go to aka.ms/olksideload. Outlook on the web opens, and after a few seconds the Add-Ins for Outlook window appears.
  3. Select My add-ins, scroll to Custom Addins at the bottom, then Add a custom add-in›Add from File. Choose the file you downloaded and accept the prompts.
  4. Restart New Outlook, or refresh Outlook on the web.
  5. Set up your signature. Start a new email: a banner asks you to Finish your Versatex signature. Select Open settings, check your name, add your title and phone, and save.

    You can come back any time: in a new email, open Apps›Versatex settings.

Every feature has an on/off switch in Versatex settings.

Good to know

  • Works in Outlook on the web and New Outlook for Windows. Emails sent from the Outlook phone app go out unstyled.
  • Recipients don't need anything installed: the styling is part of the email, and it holds up in classic Outlook and on phones.
  • Your quoted thread is never changed; only the part you wrote.
  • To remove it, open aka.ms/olksideload, go to My add-ins›Custom Addins, choose … next to Versatex Email Style, then Remove.
